Privacy Policy — tts-server-proxy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

In one sentence: tts-server-proxy does not collect, store, or share any of your data with the developer or any third party. All speech synthesis happens on a server you configure, on your network.

What the app does

tts-server-proxy is an Android text-to-speech (TTS) engine. When any Android app asks the system to speak text out loud, this app sends that text to a speech-synthesis server whose address you configure yourself (for example a Qwen3-TTS server running on your own computer or LAN), receives the generated audio, and plays it through the normal Android audio pipeline.

Data the developer collects

None. The app contains no analytics, no telemetry, no advertising SDKs, no crash-reporting services, and it never contacts the developer or any third-party server on its own. It only talks to the endpoint you enter in its settings.

Data processed on your behalf

  • Text to synthesize: sent, in the moment of synthesis, to the server endpoint you configured. If that server is on your LAN, the text stays on your LAN.
  • Server endpoint and API key: stored only in the app's private local storage on your device. They are sent solely as authentication to your server and to nobody else.

The developer of this app operates none of these servers and has no access to them.

Permissions

  • INTERNET — to reach the speech server you configured.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE / FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK — to keep synthesis alive while it runs (Android may otherwise freeze background apps).
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — optional, to show a small "synthesizing…" progress notification. The app works if you deny it.

Network security

The app speaks plain HTTP by design, because it is meant for self-hosted servers on a trusted local network. If your endpoint supports HTTPS, you can enter an https:// address and the app will use it. Use endpoints you control or trust: whatever you type is sent to the server you chose.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy ever changes (it shouldn't — the app has no way to phone home), the updated version will be published at this same address with a new date.
